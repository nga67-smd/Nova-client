Everything a launcher should do

Install, tune and play without leaving one window.

Every loader

Vanilla, Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge. OptiFine works with the installer you supply.

Separate instances

Each one has its own mods, settings, Java and memory. Duplicate, export and import them.

Automatic Java

Nova finds Java on your PC or downloads the right version for each Minecraft release.

Smart memory

RAM is suggested from your system, the loader and how many mods you run.

JVM presets

Nova Balanced, Aikar's flags, Generational ZGC, Low-end PC, or your own arguments.

Performance pack

One click adds Sodium, Lithium and friends, plus Performance, Balanced and Quality option profiles.

Two mod sites, one search

Find mods, shaders and resource packs on Modrinth and CurseForge from the Discover tab.

  • Dependencies are installed for you.
  • Update checks show what has a newer version.
  • Clean-up clears old logs, crash reports and caches.

Looks like you

Pick from 27 themes or set your own colors, corner roundness, text size and density. Use an image, GIF or video as the background.

  • Starfield, glow and animations switch off one by one.
  • Save your look and switch back any time.

Nova cosmetics

Design hats, face gear, chest emblems, boots and a cape that carry the Nova mark, in your own colors.

  • Skin parts are painted onto your skin and can be saved to your Minecraft account for everyone online to see.
  • Capes can't be uploaded to Minecraft, so the Nova in-game mod shows yours on your own character.
  • Your skin is loaded automatically as the starting point.
  • Discord can show "Playing Nova Launcher" with your instance and version.

Get Nova

The download is a single Nova.exe. There is nothing to install.

  1. Download Nova.exeThe button always gets the newest build. Nova-debug.exe is the same app with a console, for troubleshooting.
  2. Run itThe exe isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Add a profile and create an instanceChoose a Minecraft version and loader. Offline profiles work straight away.

Download Nova.exe

Playing online needs a Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition, and a one-time sign-in setup that is described in the project readme. Offline profiles cover single-player and offline-mode servers.
